# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

College admissions readers reviewing Mahmoud Yassin’s activities, honors and projects in English or Egyptian Arabic.

## Product Purpose

Make the submitted application’s evidence easy to review during the admissions period. Preserve the other audience implementations for later use.

## Brand Personality

Thoughtful, technical, curious. Use a cohesive AI-inspired visual identity with the original blue animated particle field, strong sans-serif typography, real project imagery and a consistent header and footer.

## Anti-references

Avoid exaggerated impact, unsupported metrics, decorative animation that obscures evidence, and repetitive marketing copy.

## Design Principles

- Match application and dossier claims, including uncertainty and attribution.
- Describe individual responsibility separately from platform totals.
- Publish no essay text, private family details or money figures.
- Make project evidence reachable without an audience gate.
- Organize content into distinct chapters, a dated timeline and a section navigator.
- Keep interactions useful, accessible and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve English and Egyptian Arabic with RTL, keyboard focus, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support.
